To find the value of \( m \) such that the line passing through points \( A(m, 2) \) and \( B(-m, 2m - 1) \) has an inclination angle of \( 45^\circ \), we start by noting that the slope of such a line is \( \tan(45^\circ) = 1 \).

\[
\text{slope} = \frac{(2m - 1) - 2}{-m - m} = \frac{2m - 3}{-2m} = \frac{3 - 2m}{2m}
\]

\[
\frac{3 - 2m}{2m} = 1
\]

\[
