1. **Curved Surface Area of a Hemisphere**: This is half of the surface area of a full sphere.
   \[
   \text{Curved surface area} = \frac{1}{2} \times 4\pi r^2 = 2\pi r^2
   \]

   \[
   \text{Curved surface area} = 2\pi (8)^2 = 2\pi \times 64 = 128\pi \, \text{cm}^2

   \[
   \text{Base area} = \pi r^2 = \pi (8)^2 = 64\pi \, \text{cm}^2
   \]

3. **Total Surface Area**: Sum of the curved surface area and the base area.
   \[
   \text{Total surface area} = 128\pi + 64\pi = 192\pi \, \text{cm}^2
