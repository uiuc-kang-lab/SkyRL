   - Number of such numbers: \(5 \times 5 \times 5 = 125\).

2. **Positions 2, 3, 4 are 1**: The thousands place (position 1) must be a non-zero digit (1-5), and the other three positions are fixed as 1. Each of these non-zero positions has 5 options (1-5), but since we are fixing positions 2,3,4 as 1, we only vary the thousands place.
   - Number of such numbers: \(5 \times 1 \times 1 \times 1 = 5\). However, since any of the three positions (2,3,4) can be 1, we multiply by 3.
   - Total: \(3 \times 5 = 15\).
