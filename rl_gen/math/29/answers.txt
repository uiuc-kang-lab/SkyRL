To find \( P(\xi > 2) \) given that \( \xi \) follows a normal distribution \( N(0, \sigma^2) \) and \( P(-2 \leq \xi \leq 2) = 0.4 \), we can use symmetry and the properties of the normal distribution.

1. The total probability is 1, so the probability outside the interval [-2, 2] is \( 1 - 0.4 = 0.6 \).
2. By symmetry, the probability to the left of -2 is equal to the probability to the right of 2.
3. Therefore, each tail has probability \( 0.6 / 2 = 0.3 \).

Thus, \( P(\xi > 2) = 0.3 \).
