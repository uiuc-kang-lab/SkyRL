The area of the region \( T' \) after applying the given matrix to \( T \) can be found by calculating the determinant of the matrix, which scales the area by its absolute value. 

Given the matrix:
\[
A = \begin{pmatrix}
3 & -1 \\
5 & 2
\end{pmatrix}
\]

The determinant of \( A \) is:
\[
\det(A) = (3)(2) - (-1)(5) = 6 + 5 = 11
\]

Thus, the area of \( T' \) is the area of \( T \) multiplied by the absolute value of the determinant:
\[
\text{Area of } T' = 15 \times |11| = 15 \times 11 = 165
\]
