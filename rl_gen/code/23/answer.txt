### Approach
The key insight is to recognize that the operations allowed are:
1. Appending a character to either sequence.
2. Copying the entire content of one sequence to the other.

The optimal strategy involves minimizing the number of appends and leveraging the copy operation as much as possible. The minimal number of operations can be determined by checking if one sequence is a prefix of the other. If one sequence is a prefix of the other, the number of operations needed is the length of the longer sequence. Otherwise, the number of operations needed is the sum of the lengths of both sequences.
